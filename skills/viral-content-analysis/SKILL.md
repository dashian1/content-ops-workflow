# 爆款内容分析

Use when a human has selected a piece of viral content and wants to extract reusable operating assets.

Required dimensions:

- 话题
- 选题
- 受众群体
- 呈现形式
- 爆款元素
- 内容结构
- 金句表达
- 剪辑风格
- 产品承接
- 风险点

Do not copy the viral content. Extract mechanics that can be migrated.
