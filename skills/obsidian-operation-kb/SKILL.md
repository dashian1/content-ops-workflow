# Obsidian 运营知识沉淀

Every note must include YAML frontmatter.

Recommended fields:

- title
- type
- status
- platform
- source_url
- product
- candidate_id
- style
- template
- source_note
- created
- tags

Keep notes small enough to reuse:

- 爆款分析 is the source record.
- Topic, angle, structure, quote, and editing cards are reusable assets.
- Product match notes connect viral mechanics to product facts.
- Performance reviews close the loop.
